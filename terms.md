# scoopclipper Terms of Service

Last updated: November 15, 2026

scoopclipper is a personal tool run by Cooper Hoy ("scoop", "the operator"). It turns livestream recordings into short video clips and, when the operator chooses, uploads them to social media accounts the operator owns (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube).

## Who may use it

scoopclipper is operated for the operator's own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public, and no one else can sign up for it or use it to post to their accounts.

## Use of connected platforms

The app acts only on accounts whose owner signed in and approved access on the platform's own consent screen, and only within the permissions granted. All use must follow the rules of each platform, including the [TikTok Terms of Service](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service), the [Meta Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/), the [Instagram Terms of Use](https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). On TikTok, uploads go to the account owner's inbox as drafts, and the account owner decides whether to publish them.

The operator is responsible for the clips the app creates and posts, and only posts content they own or have the right to use. The app does not claim any ownership of content.
