# scoopclipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

scoopclipper is a personal tool run by Cooper Hoy ("scoop"). It creates short video clips from livestreams and posts them to social media accounts that the operator owns, including the Scoopctl Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account.

## What data the app uses

The app connects only to accounts owned by its operator. It uses access tokens granted by the account owner through Facebook Login to upload videos and captions and to read basic account details (Page name and ID, Instagram username and ID) needed to post. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from other Facebook or Instagram users, and it does not read messages, comments, followers, or friends.

## How data is stored

Access tokens and app settings are stored only on the operator's own computer or server and are never published or shared with third parties. Video clips and their captions are stored on the operator's own storage until they are posted.

## Data deletion

The app holds no data about other users. To revoke the app's access to your own account, remove "scoopclipper" in Facebook under Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations. The operator can also delete any stored tokens on request.

## Contact

For questions or deletion requests, message the Scoopctl Facebook Page or open an issue at https://github.com/34coopatroopa/scoopclipper/issues
