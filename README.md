# scoopclipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

scoopclipper is a personal tool run by Cooper Hoy ("scoop"). It creates short video clips from livestreams and posts them to social media accounts that the operator owns: the Scoopctl Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account, the operator's TikTok account and the operator's YouTube channel.

See also the [Terms of Service](terms.html).

## What data the app uses

The app connects only to accounts owned by its operator, and only after the account owner signs in and approves access on the platform's own consent screen.

- **Facebook and Instagram (Facebook Login):** access tokens are used to upload videos and captions and to read basic account details (Page name and ID, Instagram username and ID) needed to post.
- **TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API):** with the `user.info.basic` and `video.upload` permissions, the app reads the account's basic profile (open ID and display name) and uploads videos to the account owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. The account owner reviews and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
- **YouTube (YouTube API Services):** with the `youtube.upload` and `youtube.readonly` permissions, the app uploads videos to the operator's channel and reads the channel name. By using the YouTube features you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from other users of these platforms, and it does not read messages, comments, followers, friends or viewing history.

## How data is stored

Access tokens and app settings are stored only on the operator's own computer or server and are never published or shared with third parties. Video clips and their captions are stored on the operator's own storage until they are posted.

## Data deletion and revoking access

The app holds no data about other users. To revoke the app's access to your own account:

- **Facebook / Instagram:** remove "scoopclipper" in Facebook under Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
- **TikTok:** remove "scoopclipper" in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
- **YouTube / Google:** remove "scoopclipper" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens. The operator can also delete any stored tokens on request.
